About UniConnect

A social network built for university students.

UniConnect brings everything on campus into one place. Every account belongs to a verified student, confirmed through their university email, so the people you meet here really are your schoolmates.

  • Feed and Media — share posts, photos and videos with your followers.
  • Communities — join groups for your courses, department, clubs and interests.
  • Messages — chat one-to-one with other students.
  • Events — see what's happening on campus and who's going.
  • Marketplace and Bookshop — buy, sell and swap with students near you.

Help

Answers to the most common questions.

How do I join?

Choose Sign Up and register with your matric number and university email. We send a link to that email; open it to switch your account on.

How do I sign in?

Type either your email address or your matric number in the first box, then your password.

I didn't get the verification email

Check your spam folder, and make sure you typed your university email correctly when you registered.

I forgot my password

Use Forgot password? on the sign-in page. We'll email you a link to choose a new one.

What does "Remember me" do?

When it's ticked you stay signed in on this device. Untick it on a shared computer and you'll be signed out when the browser closes.

Privacy

What UniConnect keeps about you, and why.

What we store

Your name, matric number, university email, university, faculty, department and level, plus anything you add yourself: your profile, posts, photos, videos, comments, messages, listings and community activity.

Who can see it

Your posts and profile are shown to other signed-in students according to your account settings. Messages are visible only to the people in the conversation. Your password is stored scrambled (hashed), so no one can read it.

How it's used

Only to run UniConnect: to show your content, confirm you're a student, and email you about your account (such as verification and password reset links). We don't sell your information.

Removing your data

You can delete your own posts at any time. To remove your whole account, contact the UniConnect administrators.

Terms

The ground rules for using UniConnect.

Your account

UniConnect is for current students. Register only with your own matric number and university email, keep your password private, and don't let anyone else use your account.

Be respectful

Don't post anything that harasses, threatens or impersonates someone, that is hateful or sexually explicit, or that breaks your university's rules or the law.

Your content

What you post stays yours. By posting it you let UniConnect show it to other students on the platform. Only share things you have the right to share.

Marketplace and Bookshop

Sales are between students. UniConnect doesn't handle payments or guarantee items, so meet in safe, public places on campus.

Breaking the rules

Content that breaks these terms may be removed, and accounts that keep breaking them may be suspended.
