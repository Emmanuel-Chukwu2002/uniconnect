Help & Support

Quick answers, and where to read more.

How do I change my department or university?

These were verified with your university email when you signed up, so they can't be changed here. If they're wrong, contact the UniConnect administrators.

Why do people need my approval to follow me?

Every follow on UniConnect is a request you approve, so only people you accept see your followers-only activity. Review them in Follow requests.

I'm getting too many notifications

Turn off the kinds you don't want in Notifications.

I think someone else is using my account

Change your password in Privacy & Security; that signs out every other device straight away.
